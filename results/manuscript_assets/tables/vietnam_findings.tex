The daily workbooks contain 987 pond-day grid rows across twelve year--pond series (six pond labels per year). There are 404 observed DO and 413 observed pH entries in 2018, and 446 DO and 473 pH entries in 2019. Templates continue beyond some crops; blank trailing dates are not invented observations.

Validation-only selection chooses persistence for both daily tasks: 2019 DO MAE 0.2128 on 441 eligible forecasts, and pH MAE 0.1945 on 468. Ridge has a lower post-hoc pH point MAE than persistence, but this marginal comparison does not quantify a paired advantage and it was not chosen by the frozen validation rule; the test winner is not relabelled as the selected model. The stricter domain-QC sensitivity leaves both selected model names unchanged. Selected persistence needs no learned coefficient payload, while still requiring sampling and state storage.
